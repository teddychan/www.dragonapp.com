Dictation keyboard for iOS

HandFree for iPhone

Switch to the HandFree keyboard, hold the mic, and speak. Lift your finger to put the transcript where you're typing.

Availability

iOS 26 or later · English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese · Internal beta

See it in action

Speak from the keyboard

The first tap opens HandFree to get the mic ready. Return to the app you were using, then hold the mic to dictate.

Type between recordings

Switch between English, Pinyin, Cangjie, and emoji on the same keyboard.

Start with on-device speech

Apple's speech recognition runs on your iPhone by default. Add your own provider key if you want cloud recognition or AI text cleanup.

Availability

HandFree for iPhone is in internal TestFlight testing. Public downloads are unavailable.
